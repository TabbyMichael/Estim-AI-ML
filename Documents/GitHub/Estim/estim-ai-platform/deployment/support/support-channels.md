# Estim AI Platform - Support Channels

## Primary Channels
- `#estim-support`: user questions, access issues, how-to help.
- `#estim-incidents`: active P1/P2 coordination only.
- `#estim-deployments`: release and maintenance-window notices.
- Email `support@estim.ai`: tracked requests, SLA starts on receipt.
- Status page `https://status.estim.ai`: outage and maintenance notices.

## Hours and Response Targets
- Business hours: 09:00–18:00 local, Monday–Friday.
- P1: acknowledge in 15 minutes, update every 30 minutes.
- P2: acknowledge in 2 hours, update every 4 hours.
- P3/P4: acknowledge next business day.

## Routing Rules
1. Support triages all incoming requests.
2. Access/billing/data issues stay with support unless unresolved in 1 business day.
3. API errors, data drift, and failed jobs escalate to platform on-call.
4. Security or suspected breach escalates immediately to P1.
