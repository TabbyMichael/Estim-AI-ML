# Estim AI Platform - Post-Launch Support

## Support Channels
- Definition and routing live in `deployment/support/support-channels.md`.
- Use `#estim-support` for help, `#estim-incidents` for active P1/P2 work.
- Publish outages and maintenance notices on the status page.

## Issue Tracking
- Follow `deployment/support/issue-tracking.md`.
- Require environment, reproduction steps, request IDs, and severity labels.
- Close issues only after staging verification and requester confirmation.

## Escalation
- Follow `deployment/support/escalation-process.md`.
- Support → on-call → engineering/data owners → platform lead/security.
- Include impact, recent changes, logs, monitor reports, and next owner.

## Training
- Follow `deployment/support/support-training.md`.
- Complete role walkthroughs, guided exercises, shadow shifts, and runbook sign-off.
